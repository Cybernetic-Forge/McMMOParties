[HEADING=2][COLOR=#27CDD3]Why McMMOParties?[/COLOR][/HEADING]
[B]McMMOParties expands traditional mcMMO groups into a true shared progression experience.[/B]

[QUOTE]
Level up together, unlock party-wide perks, improve utility over time, and connect your group system with economy and dungeon gameplay. McMMOParties is designed for servers that want parties to feel like a real long-term progression layer instead of a simple invite list.
[/QUOTE]

[B][SIZE=5][COLOR=#27CDD3]Best for[/COLOR][/SIZE][/B]
RPG, survival, and MMO-inspired Paper networks using mcMMO.

[B][SIZE=5][COLOR=#27CDD3]What players feel[/COLOR][/SIZE][/B]
A stronger sense of teamwork through shared progression, unlockable quality-of-life perks, and party identity that grows over time.

[HR][/HR]

[HEADING=2][COLOR=#27CDD3]Core Features[/COLOR][/HEADING]

[TABLE]
[TR]
[TD][B]Shared party progression[/B]
Party EXP is earned from mcMMO skill activity and scaled through a configurable level curve.[/TD]
[TD][B]Flexible progression model[/B]
Use the modern skillpoint system or keep a legacy level-based unlock flow.[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD][B]Upgradeable perk tree[/B]
Increase EXP sharing rate, sharing radius, member slots, dungeon instance slots, treasury size, ability duration, and cooldown reduction.[/TD]
[TD][B]Unlockable party utility[/B]
Progression can unlock party chat, party treasury access, and party waypoint access.[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD][B]Private and configurable parties[/B]
Support open parties, invite-only parties, and password-protected joining rules.[/TD]
[TD][B]GUI-driven experience[/B]
Players can use party overview and list interfaces instead of relying on plain command output.[/TD]
[/TR]
[TR]
[TD][B]Cross-server ready[/B]
Includes Velocity messaging support for party chat and teleport-related network communication.[/TD]
[TD][B]Storage and administration[/B]
Works with SQLite, MySQL, and MariaDB, and includes admin tools for reloads and party management.[/TD]
[/TR]
[/TABLE]
